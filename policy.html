Before You Visit

Salon Policy

Please take a moment to read our policies before booking your appointment.

Guarantee

Regular nail polish services carry no guarantee. Acrylic, Shellac, and Gel services are guaranteed for 72 hours — free repairs are available for any salon-caused issues within this window.

The salon is not responsible for damage or infections that occur after your visit.

Cancellation

We kindly ask that you provide at least 24 hours' notice if you need to reschedule or cancel your appointment. This allows us to offer your slot to another client.

No Show / No Call

After 3 missed appointments without notice, clients will be required to either walk in or book with a non-refundable deposit paid in full prior to the appointment.

Late Arrival

Please contact us if you are running late. We will always do our best to accommodate you, however we cannot guarantee your full service if you arrive more than 10 minutes after your scheduled time.

Refund

Once services have been paid for, we consider that you have accepted and are happy with the completed result. We do not offer refunds after payment has been made.

Children

Children under 12 must be accompanied and supervised by a responsible adult at all times. Parents and guardians are liable for any damages caused by children in the salon.

Gift Vouchers

Gift vouchers are non-refundable, non-transferable, and cannot be exchanged for cash. Please present your voucher at the time of your appointment.

Service Refusal

RDS Nails & Beauty reserves the right to refuse service to any client presenting with open wounds, contagious skin or nail conditions, or who displays inappropriate behaviour toward our staff.

Health & Allergies

Please inform us of any allergies or sensitivities before your appointment. If you experience any adverse reaction or infection following a visit, please contact us immediately.

The salon is not responsible for lost or damaged personal belongings.

By booking an appointment with RDS Nails & Beauty, you agree to the above policies. Thank you for your understanding — we look forward to welcoming you.
